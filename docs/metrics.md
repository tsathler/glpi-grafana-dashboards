# Metrics

## Implemented

None. The provisioned dashboard is intentionally empty during Milestone 1.

## Planned

After schema discovery and validation, the initial operational metrics are open tickets, new today, pending, unassigned, high priority, created versus resolved, backlog total and age, category, technician, and group distributions, plus a table of tickets requiring attention.

Definitions depend on verified GLPI status and relationship semantics. SLA, TTO, and TTR are later scope and require separate validation. No assumptions or SQL are encoded yet.
